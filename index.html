About Me

I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Innsbruck under the supervision of Prof. Birgit Schörkhuber. My research focuses on Calculus of Variations and Partial Differential Equations, particularly nonlinear Schrödinger equations and related elliptic problems.
